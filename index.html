🎬 We wanted the real Liam Neeson. Budget was zero dollars and one candy wrapper.

A whole three-ring circus was going off the rails in my life, and I wanted to challenge myself this summer, and put myself out there and meet new people. I was pretty sure I was gonna be kidnapped and held for ransom, but I’m very glad that I bumped into you. You’ve helped me in more ways than you know, and I’ll always be grateful to you for that.

You’re one of the best things that happened to me this summer. Except the time I found a quarter in my jacket when doing laundry. Turns out it was a candy wrap. But you’re a solid close second, no cap.

🎬 The quarter remains at large. Investigation ongoing. The candy wrapper has been questioned and released.
